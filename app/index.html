    Roadmap to the platinum

    Difficulty 4 / 10About 200 hours1 playthrough0 online trophies

    1. Step 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

      Finish Chapters 1 to 5 and take the optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with binoculars as you go — the 100% checklist already counts that progress, even though the checklist itself only unlocks after the story. Do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house (see the warning above). Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter: there are 15 manual slots plus one autosave, and no mission replay. Keep cheats off — they disable trophies and saving.

      Covers: the 12 story trophies, plus a head start on vehicles, animals and challenges.

    2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

      After the story, open Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests need everything; most of the rest is partial — 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 100 optional missions, 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditations, 15 of 18 card sets, 300 of 397 stunts. It is closer to 60% true completion than 100%.

      Covers: A Long Road (gold), Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, card sets, WAiNK scans, challenges, vehicles, map exploration, money and garages.

    3. Step 3 — Cleanup about 10 hours

      Strangers & Freaks strands and everything left over. Every quest giver is available again in free-roam after the story, so work through the stranger questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), the robberies, the plane and tank steals, and the odd one-offs like the UFO and the bowling alley.

      Covers: the stranger questlines, Strangers & Freaks, the robbery trophies, and the miscellaneous activities.
